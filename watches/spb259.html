Ginza

SPB259J1

1959 Alpinist Modern Re-interpretation · 2021

◆ LIMITED EDITION

1 of 3,500 produced

Seiko says the blue-grey dial, with two overlaid sunray patterns, captures the cobblestones of Ginza, Tokyo (where Seiko began), and the seconds hand is in Komparu colour, a traditional blue-green named after Ginza's Komparu Street.
